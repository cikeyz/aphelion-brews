What's Playing

Aphelion doesn't play music loudly enough to need Shazam. It plays it loudly enough to feel held.

Resident sound: slow ambient folk into lo-fi soul into soft electronic. The progression that happens naturally between 8 PM and midnight in a place where no one's rushing you.

Weekly Events

Every Friday

Slow Frequency

8 – 11 PM

A vinyl DJ set by a rotating local artist. No talking over the music. No requests. The DJ chooses, and you trust them. Entrance: the price of one drink.

Every Other Wednesday

Static & Verse

7:30 PM

Open mic for spoken word and poetry. Not a slam. Not a competition. A sharing. Two-minute maximum per piece. Applause is soft - we snap fingers here. Sign-up sheet on the counter; 8 slots per night.

Every Saturday

Lo-fi Studio Mornings

9 AM – 12 PM

No events. No talking unless necessary. Just ambient music, coffee, and whatever you're working on. Some people come every week. We know their order.

Work Here

We're small. Everyone who works here matters a lot.

Signal Keeper

Head Barista

You read the room before it asks anything of you, and you pull espresso like it's architecture - purposeful, precise, irreversible. We're looking for someone who treats every drink as a small piece of care made tangible, and who finds genuine meaning in getting it exactly right.

Drift Companion

Floor & Vibe (Part-time)

Part host, part ambient presence. You make people feel seen without making it a performance. You refill water without being asked and choose the right song for 9 PM on a Tuesday. We're looking for someone who understands that hospitality is quiet love, not theater.

Frequency Curator

Events & Music Coordinator (Part-time)

You build a Friday night from nothing - choosing the DJ, the lighting cue, the opening record. You run Static & Verse like a gentle conductor. You understand that programming a space is an act of emotional architecture, and you take it seriously without taking yourself seriously.
