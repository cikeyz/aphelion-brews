Come Work Here

We're small. Everyone who works here matters a lot.

Working at Aphelion means being treated like someone with taste, because we hired you assuming you have it. We don't do toxic positivity, performative culture, or mandatory fun.

Brew Architect

Barista / Drinks Lead

Own every drink in The Pour. Maintain equipment calibration. Carry consistency across every shift. You have opinions about extraction ratios and you're right about most of them.

Table Keeper

Front-of-House

Manage service from order to close. Handle the room with enough attention that guests feel looked after and enough restraint that they don't feel watched.

Provisions Curator

Sourcing & Inventory

Source and manage all ingredients - from coffee lots to agar to beeswax. Maintain quality standards and the relationships that make them possible.

Visual Correspondent

Brand & Content

Own Aphelion's visual presence: photography, layout, seasonal copy, and collateral that looks effortless because you put in the effort before anyone arrived.

How to apply

No forms. No cover letters. Write us at orbit@aphelion.cafe - tell us what you'd play at 10 PM on a quiet Tuesday.
