Precision drinks. Unhurried space. Nothing extra.

Aphelion started as a question: what does a drink shop look like when the person making it actually cares about what goes in the cup? Not care in the marketing sense - care in the sourcing, the calibration, the 48-hour cold steep and the cup that's been warmed before your order is even called. We built this place for the people who notice the difference, and said nothing about it because they didn't need to.

What we do

  • Specialty coffee and tea, traceable to lot
  • Light meals and grounded plates
  • Small-batch snacks and pastries
  • Curated vinyl sets and listening hours

The Frequency

The room should sound soft before it sounds loud.

The lights at Aphelion sit at 70% of full - warm, directional, never overhead. The speakers are flush-mounted and the acoustics were tuned for conversation, not performance; you'll hear the music the way you hear rain when you're already inside. The playlist doesn't theme itself around the brand. It themes itself around the hour - something slower before noon, something with more presence in the afternoon, something almost too quiet by close. If you've been here long enough to notice the shift, you've been here long enough.

Come Work Here

Working at Aphelion means being treated like someone with taste, because we hired you assuming you have it.

We don't do toxic positivity, performative culture, or mandatory fun - we do quiet excellence, clear expectations, and a team that actually covers for each other.

Brew Architect

Barista / Drinks Lead

Who you are: You have opinions about extraction ratios and you're right about most of them. You're patient in explanation and rigorous in execution - the kind of person who cleans the portafilter before the next shot, without being asked.

What you'll do: Own the full execution of every drink in The Pour category, maintain all equipment calibration, and carry the consistency of Aphelion's drink standard across every single shift.

Table Keeper

Front-of-House / Hospitality

Who you are: You read a room without staring at it - you know when someone wants conversation and when they need to be left alone, and you respect both with equal care.

What you'll do: Manage service from order to close, handle the room with enough attention that guests feel looked after and enough restraint that they don't feel watched.

Provisions Curator

Sourcing & Inventory

Who you are: You're the person who finds the Benguet supplier six months before anyone else does, and keeps a spreadsheet that is, inexplicably, beautiful.

What you'll do: Source and manage all ingredients and materials - from coffee lots to agar to beeswax - maintaining both quality standards and the supply relationships that make them possible.

Visual Correspondent

Brand & Content

Who you are: You shoot with natural light by instinct, not instruction, and you know the difference between a caption that sells and a caption that performs - and you only write the former.

What you'll do: Own Aphelion's visual presence across all channels: photography, layout, seasonal copy, and the occasional piece of collateral that looks like it took less effort than it did.

How to apply

No forms. No cover letters. Write us at orbit@aphelion.cafe - tell us what you'd play at 10 PM on a quiet Tuesday.
